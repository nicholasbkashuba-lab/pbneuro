Serving Palm Beach Gardens

Neurology Care for Palm Beach Gardens

Comprehensive neurological care for Palm Beach Gardens — from the PGA Boulevard corridor to the Gardens Mall — delivered a short drive south at our West Palm Beach office.

Palm Beach Gardens is a master-planned north-county city built around an active, outdoor way of life — the golf communities near PGA National, the offices and restaurants along PGA Boulevard, and the shopping and gathering spots at the Gardens Mall. Its residents, from busy professionals to comfortable retirees, expect specialist care that keeps pace with them. When a neurological concern surfaces, Palm Beach Neurology & Premiere Research Institute is a short drive south in West Palm Beach — subspecialty attention close to home, with no long trek to Miami or Orlando.

The concerns that bring Palm Beach Gardens neighbors to us reach across every stage of life: the migraines that derail a workday, the memory changes an adult child first notices in a parent, the tremor or slowed movement that may point to Parkinson's, the tingling and numbness of neuropathy, and the seizures, MS flares, and post-stroke questions that call for an expert's eye. In keeping with our motto, we treat the patient, not just the disease — with time to listen and a plan built around your life.

Caring for Palm Beach Gardens

Our physicians bring national-level expertise to north-county patients. Those living with headache and migraine can see Paul Winner, DO, FAAN, FAHS, a nationally recognized headache leader, or Arnaldo Da Silva, MD, FAHS; families facing memory loss meet with Carl Sadowsky, MD, FAAN; tremor and movement concerns are evaluated by Michael Alosilla, MD; and nerve or muscle symptoms fall to Reed Stone, MD, FAAN. On-site testing and treatment include EEG, EMG and nerve-conduction studies, cognitive and memory testing, Botox for chronic migraine and spasticity, infusion therapy, and deep brain stimulation programming — plus a FREE Memory Screen and, through our on-site Premiere Research Institute, access to monitored clinical trials in Alzheimer's, migraine, and multiple sclerosis. Meet our physicians to find the right fit.

Getting to our office

Reaching us from Palm Beach Gardens is straightforward: our office at 4631 N Congress Ave in West Palm Beach sits a short drive south and is easily reached from the PGA Boulevard area via I-95 or Congress Avenue. We see patients Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Friday until 4:30 PM, and we are accepting new patients. Call 561-845-0500 to schedule, or request an appointment online.

Is there a neurologist near Palm Beach Gardens?

Yes. Palm Beach Neurology & Premiere Research Institute is a short drive south of Palm Beach Gardens at 4631 N Congress Ave in West Palm Beach, easily reached via I-95 or Congress Avenue from the PGA Boulevard area. You get subspecialty neurology care close to home. Call 561-845-0500 to schedule.

What neurological conditions do you treat for Palm Beach Gardens residents?

We care for headache and migraine, memory loss and Alzheimer's, Parkinson's and movement disorders, epilepsy and seizures, multiple sclerosis, stroke and TIA, neuropathy, sleep disorders, neck and back pain, and more. On-site testing includes EEG, EMG and nerve-conduction studies, and cognitive and memory testing.

Is the Memory Screen really free?

Yes. We offer a no-cost Memory Screen that takes about 30 minutes — a simple first step if you or a loved one in Palm Beach Gardens has noticed memory changes. Call 561-845-0500 to arrange one.
