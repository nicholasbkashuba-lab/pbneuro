Appointment Options

Two Ways to Begin

Free · 30 min

FREE Memory Screen

A brief, no-cost screening designed to assess memory and cognitive function and identify potential memory problems or early signs of conditions like mild cognitive impairment (MCI) or dementia — simple, structured tests of memory, attention, and thinking skills.

⏱ 30 min

New patients welcome

New Patient Appointment

A comprehensive first visit to develop a complete medical profile, identify any risk factors, and create a personalized care plan. Please bring a list of current medications, past medical records, previous test results, and insurance information.

⏱ 1 hour

Fill out your new patient paperwork before your appointment to save time in the waiting room. Questions about any of the forms? Call our office at 561-845-0500.
